A practical job application checklist

Use this checklist to prepare accurate applications, avoid repeating work and keep a useful record of what happens next.

On this page
  1. Verify the opportunity
  2. Prepare your information
  3. Review before submitting
  4. Record the outcome

1. Verify the opportunity

  • Open the role on the employer’s official careers site when possible. Check that the title, location and requisition match the listing you found.
  • Read the requirements, work location, schedule and employment type. Separate essential qualifications from preferences.
  • Check whether you already applied to the same requisition. Different links can lead to the same role.
  • Be cautious of requests to pay to apply or provide banking credentials. Verify unusual requests through the employer’s official contact channels.

2. Prepare accurate information

  • Check your contact details and use a resume version that accurately describes relevant experience.
  • Have employment dates, education and portfolio links ready. Do not invent qualifications to match a job description.
  • Prepare answers to recurring questions, but reread each question before reusing an answer. A similar-looking question may ask something different.
  • Read what will happen to uploaded information. Share only what is needed and avoid adding sensitive identifiers to free-text answers.

3. Review before submitting

  • Inspect every required field and the uploaded file. Autofill can put a correct answer in the wrong place.
  • Check employer-specific questions, locations and dates again. Make sure the selected options reflect your actual situation.
  • Read declarations and agreements yourself. Complete identity checks, sign-in prompts and other sensitive steps directly.
  • Distinguish a review screen from a submission confirmation. A button click alone does not establish that the employer received the application.

4. Record the outcome and next step

  • Save the role title, employer, direct URL, requisition ID if available and the date you applied.
  • Keep the employer’s confirmation or application reference. If confirmation is missing, check the employer portal before submitting again.
  • Note which resume and answers you used so you can prepare consistently for an interview.
  • Track any next action and follow the employer’s stated timeline. Record interviews, rejections and offers based on actual communications.
