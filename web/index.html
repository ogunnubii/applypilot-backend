next application.

Know what happens at each step.

What happens when an application gets stuck?

It appears in your workspace with the blocker and available actions. You can enter missing information or take over the employer form.

Does it solve human verification for me?

Human verification and sign-in can require your action. Use the live browser or complete the step directly through the desktop Chrome extension.

Can I use ApplyPilot on my phone?

Yes, the dashboard supports phones. The Chrome extension currently requires a desktop computer; it does not run in mobile Chrome.

Does the extension transfer my cloud browser?

No. It rebuilds the form from saved details in a new local tab. Unsaved edits and verification from the cloud session do not transfer.

How do I know an application was submitted?

Look for an employer receipt. ApplyPilot detects supported confirmation messages and also lets you record a receipt manually. Opening a form or clicking Submit alone is not a confirmed submission.
