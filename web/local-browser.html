Apply from your normal Chrome or Edge profile

ApplyPilot uses your existing browser logins to fill saved facts, advance routine forms and submit when no human step is needed.

Download Chrome / Edge extension

  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep. In chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. Select the folder containing manifest.json.
  2. Choose Hosted ApplyPilot in the popup, or This PC if you started the Windows backend on localhost:8080. Use the backend's /setup page to save your profile/resume and choose My Windows browser.
  3. Keep ApplyPilot signed in and open. Review your Found jobs, then click Start routine applications. You can also open an individual job manually or enable its routine steps.
  4. When blocked, complete CAPTCHA/MFA, unknown facts or legal statements yourself. The queue continues to other jobs. Use Remember an answer for exact questions you want reused.

Submitted requires an observed employer receipt after a submission action. Uncertain submissions never automatically retry. You can record an actual employer receipt in the dashboard.

Supported host families now include Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, BambooHR and Recruitee. Custom widgets, embedded forms and unfamiliar redirects may need manual handling. This is conservative form support, not guaranteed automation for every ATS variant.

After restarting Windows, restart your local backend if applicable, sign in, and review unfinished jobs before starting another run. Queue records persist; automation stops on browser startup. Cloud cookies and unsaved form edits do not transfer.
