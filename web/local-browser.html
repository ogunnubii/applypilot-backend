ApplyPilot uses your existing browser logins to fill saved facts into the open form. You control Next and every Submit click.

What is new in 0.6.18

Autofill-only mode watches for newly appearing fields. Repeated manual Submit clicks are no longer blocked or replayed. Paused employer forms also check for newly saved answers every 30 seconds and fill them without advancing or submitting. Combined first-and-last-name prompts now use your saved full name. Saved-answer refill now reconnects existing forms without stalling. Visible labels take priority over generic field names, unapproved captured answers no longer suppress approved facts, and delayed forms have time to load. Gemini requests get a longer response window. Reload your existing extension and reopen ApplyPilot to use these fixes.

ApplyPilot now handles more multi-step forms and exact saved answers in custom dropdowns, radios and checkboxes. It fills supported fields without clicking Next or Submit. Submission tracking observes your clicks without blocking the employer page.

Recognizes employer 503 and other error pages before filling. For a newly opened, empty application page in automatic mode, it waits about a minute and tries one fresh page load. Other employer sites can continue. Forms already filled, existing applications, and submission attempts are never automatically reloaded. A repeated outage stays paused with a clear explanation.

Update your existing extension

Extract the new ZIP over the existing extension folder, then click Reload in chrome://extensions or edge://extensions. Keep the same browser profile and installation so recorded attempts remain protected. Refresh the signed-in dashboard after reloading.

  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder you will keep. In chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. Select the folder containing manifest.json.
  2. Choose Hosted ApplyPilot in the popup, or This PC if you started the Windows backend on localhost:8080. Use the backend's /setup page to save your profile/resume and choose My Windows browser.
  3. Keep ApplyPilot signed in and open. Queue the jobs you want to apply to or enable auto-queue on a saved search. The extension runs new queued jobs every 30 seconds. You can also open an individual job manually or enable its routine steps.
  4. When blocked, complete CAPTCHA/MFA, unknown facts or legal statements yourself. The queue continues to other jobs. Use Remember an answer for exact questions you want reused.

Submitted requires an observed employer receipt after your submission action. Uncertain submissions never automatically retry. You can record an actual employer receipt in the dashboard.

Supported host families now include Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, BambooHR and Recruitee. Custom widgets, embedded forms and unfamiliar redirects may need manual handling. This is conservative form support, not guaranteed automation for every ATS variant.

After restarting Windows, restart your local backend if applicable, sign in, and review unfinished jobs before starting another run. Queue records persist. New queued jobs can continue after sign-in; interrupted and attempted applications stay paused for review. Cloud cookies and unsaved form edits do not transfer.
